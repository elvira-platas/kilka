Reader introduction fonts

Playfair Display, Cormorant Garamond and Montserrat are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. See the accompanying family-specific OFL files for copyright and license terms.

Source: Google Fonts (https://github.com/google/fonts), retrieved 2026-09-27. WOFF2 variable files supplied by the Google Fonts CSS API; normal and italic, Latin, Latin Extended and Cyrillic subsets (including Cyrillic Extended where supplied). Font binaries are unmodified.

These files are served locally by Kilka Reader. No external font service is contacted by the visitor. CSS aliases prefixed with Kilka Intro isolate the faces from theme fonts.

Weight ranges: Playfair Display 400-900; Cormorant Garamond 300-700; Montserrat 100-900.

Ruslan Display (Google Fonts, retrieved 2026-09-28): unmodified WOFF2 Latin, Latin Extended and Cyrillic files, regular 400 only. SIL OFL 1.1; see ruslandisplay-OFL.txt. No synthetic bold or italic is used.
