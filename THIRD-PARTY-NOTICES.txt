Fonts
-----
The theme includes local font files for Roboto, Montserrat, Oswald, Playfair
Display, and Merriweather under assets/fonts/. Latin and Cyrillic subsets are
bundled locally; the theme does not request Google Fonts or any other font
resource from an external server at runtime.

Font licenses are family-specific. The font files originate from the Google
Fonts collection, and the applicable license for each family should be checked
against its upstream source before redistribution outside this project:
https://fonts.google.com/
